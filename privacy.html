Privacy

What we do with what you send

We use what you send through this site to give you a quote and to do the work. Nothing else.

What we collect

What you type into the contact form: your name, a phone number or email, the service address if you gave one, and a description of the problem. If you are an emergency, we use the number to call you back.

Why we collect it

To quote the job, to get there, and to keep a record of what was agreed. If we quote you, we keep that record for as long as the tax authorities require.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your details.
  • We do not add you to a mailing list or a dialer.
  • We do not run advertising or tracking on this site.

How long we keep it

Quotes that do not turn into work are deleted within 12 months. Work we did is kept for the records we are legally required to keep.

Cookies

None. This site sets no cookies and runs no tracking pixels.

Asking us to change or delete something

Email hello@example.com and we will correct or delete it. There is no account and no preference centre, because there is no account.
